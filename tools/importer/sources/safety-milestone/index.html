GATX Reaches One Million Safe Work Hours

October 5, 2026

GATX Rail North America's repair network has completed one million consecutive work hours without a lost-time injury, a milestone reached by the teams who inspect, maintain and repair our customers' railcars every day.

A milestone built shop by shop

The achievement spans GATX-operated repair shops and mobile repair units across North America. It reflects daily pre-shift safety briefings, hands-on training for new technicians, and a culture where every employee is expected to stop work when something doesn't look right.

Over the past year, shop teams completed thousands of tank car qualifications, routine maintenance events and repairs while keeping safety performance ahead of industry benchmarks.

Safe work and quality work go hand in hand. When our people go home safely every day, our customers get cars back that are inspected, repaired and ready to run.

Vice President, Rail Maintenance Operations

What it means for your fleet

  • Consistent inspection and repair quality across every GATX shop.
  • Fewer delays caused by safety incidents in the shop.
  • Clear status updates on service events through the customer portal.

Introducing Safety First Inspections

Building on this milestone, GATX is launching Safety First Inspections. Before any repair begins, each car receives a documented safety walk-around covering valves, fittings, safety appliances and running gear. Findings are added to the car's service event, so you can review them alongside the rest of the shop record.

Safety First Inspections are included at no additional cost for cars shopped at GATX facilities. Questions about the program can go to your GATX service team, listed on the portal home page.
